Both! We offer 1-on-1 executive coaching and team workshops customized to your organization's needs. Many clients start with 1-on-1 coaching, see results, then bring us in for team training.

All coaching can be done remotely via video call (most of our clients prefer this for convenience). We also offer in-person sessions for Bay Area clients and team workshops at your office.
